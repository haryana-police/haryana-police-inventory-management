How the App Works (After Login)

Haryana Police Inventory Management System
Runs on your own computer — usually http://localhost:3210

Before You Start — Opening the App

This app runs on the computer it is installed on. There is no online website and no internet connection is needed after the first start.

  1. Open the project folder and double-click run-local.bat (on other systems, run npm start).
  2. A black console window opens and must stay open. That window is the app server — closing it stops the app.
  3. Your browser opens automatically. If it does not, look at the App: line in the console and open that address — it is usually http://localhost:3210. If that port is already in use the server picks the next free one, so always open the exact address it prints.
  4. When you finish, click the console window and press Ctrl+C to stop the server.
Where your data lives: everything is stored in one file — local-data\db.json in the project folder. To back up, copy that file somewhere safe. To start fresh, delete it and restart the server; the app then re-seeds its default data.
Using it from a phone or another computer: start the server on the main computer, then on the other device open http://<main computer's IP>:3210. To find that IP, run ipconfig on the main computer and read IPv4 Address (for example 192.168.1.5). Both devices must be on the same office network, and the main computer must stay switched on.

1. The Main Screen

After logging in you see the app window with three main parts:

  • Left sidebar — nine screens: Dashboard, Inventory, Consumable Items, Distribution, Item Issued, Demands, Maintenance, Inspections and Reports. Click any one to switch screens.
  • Top bar — District selector, the gold Manage ▾ menu (Districts / Users / Locations), a हिन्दी / English language button, the notification bell 🔔, your name and role badge, and Logout.
  • Main area — the content of the selected screen (cards, charts, tables, filters).
  • Red dot on a sidebar item — something needs your attention there (a request to approve, a distribution assigned to you, a maintenance job). The dot disappears once you act.
  • Long lists — every record list shows up to 30 rows per page. Under the table you see “Showing X–Y of Z rows” with ◀ Prev and Next ▶ buttons and page numbers; click a number to jump straight to that page. Filtering or searching starts again from page 1.
Sidebar collapse: click the ☰ icon in the top bar to shrink / expand the sidebar — handy on small screens.

2. Dashboard

The landing screen: clickable summary cards, two charts, and two quick lists. Clicking a summary card opens a detail table of exactly what that card counted, with a search box and PDF / Excel / Word / Print export.

  • Summary cards — Total Items, Total Quantity, Low Stock Alerts, Categories, and one card per condition state: Scrap, Damaged, Good. Click any card to open its detail view.
  • Charts — Stock by Category (top 8 categories) and Condition Health (units split by condition). Both refresh as data changes.
  • Low Stock Items — items at or below their minimum level, so you know what to re-order. Columns: Item, Category, Current, Min Required, Reorder, Condition, Status — click any column heading to sort. Click View All to go to the Inventory screen.
  • Recent Activity — a live feed of what is happening in the system right now, with a Live indicator; new events appear here by themselves.
Condition states are called Good, Damaged and Scrap throughout the app.

3. Inventory

The master list of durable items and their quantities, locations and condition.

  • Top buttons — + Add Stock, Import from Excel, Documents, Manage Categories.
  • Two tabs — Item Stock (current stock) and Stock History (every stock change, with the previous and new figure).

Item Stock tab

  • Filters — a location selector (All Locations), a search box, All Categories, and All Stock / Only Issued / Only Available.
  • Columns — S.No, Item Name, Category, Total Qty, Available, Issued, Lost, Damaged, Scrap, Status, Actions. A bold Total row sits at the bottom.
  • Each row has an Actions button (view, edit, adjust stock, and more depending on your role). Clicking the blue item name opens the item's full profile (section 10).

Stock History tab

  • Filters — All Categories, All Locations, All Conditions (Good / Damaged / Scrap), plus a search box and Clear.
  • Columns — Item Name, Category, Stock Change, Unit, Min Stock, Location, Condition, Status, Last Updated, Actions.
Use Import from Excel to load many items at once from a spreadsheet, and Documents to reach the attached document list.

4. Consumable Items

Stock that gets used up — paper, fuel, cartridges and similar. It is a separate catalogue from the Inventory items, and every hand-over needs the receiving side's approval.

  • Top buttons — + Add Consume Item, Manage Categories.
  • Two tabs — Item Consume (current stock) and Consume History (the full ledger).

Item Consume tab

  • Columns — S.No, Item Name, Category, Total Qty, Available, Distributed, Lost, Status, Actions. A Total row appears at the bottom.
  • Filters — search, All Categories, All Locations, and All Stock / Only Distributed / Only Available.
  • Row actions — View, plus Distribute and Mark Lost where applicable.
  • A strip above the table shows the running figures: Total Quantity, Available (in stock now), Added/Received, Distributed and Lost.

Consume History tab

  • First table — Item Category, Item, Quantity, Date of Distribution, Distributed To, Distributed By, Status, Actions. Filters: search, All Categories, All Items, All Status (Available / Pending Approval / Complete / Rejected / Lost), All Locations, and a date range.
  • Second table, Distribution by Location — Recipient, Type, Category, Item, Quantity, Date, Distributed By, Status, Actions.

How to hand over a consumable

  1. On Item Consume, click Actions → Distribute, enter the quantity, search and pick the recipient (a unit or a staff member), then Send Distribution Request. The row now shows Pending Approval.
  2. The recipient opens Consume History and on their own row clicks Approve or Reject (a rejection reason is required). Only then does the quantity count as distributed; a rejection releases it back to Available.
  3. To record damage or wastage, use Actions → Mark Lost on the Item Consume row.
You can never distribute or mark lost more than the Available quantity — the app blocks it and shows both numbers. Only the receiving unit or person can approve or reject a request.

5. Distribution

Moving durable stock from one unit to another. The sender raises the transfer, and the named receiver must Approve it — only then is stock actually deducted from the sender and added to the receiver.

  • Top button — + Distribute Items.
  • Summary cards — Total Distributions, Completed, Pending, Rejected. Click any card to see that list.
  • Filters — search, All Status (Pending / Completed / Rejected), a date range, and Clear.
  • Columns — S.No, Item, Category, Qty, Distributed By, Distributed To, Status, Date, Actions.
  • Row actions — Approve and Reject (for the recipient), always Details. Rows you cannot act on show Awaiting recipient.

How to send stock

  1. Click + Distribute Items.
  2. Under Distributed To, open the dropdown and tick one or more recipients (or use All).
  3. Choose Type of Items — Stock or Consumable.
  4. Under Items, click + Add Item and fill in each row (category, item, quantities by condition). You can attach Photos / Attachments and add Remarks.
  5. Click Submit Distribution. One record is created per recipient, numbered like DIST-000001, with status Pending.
  6. The recipient opens the row and clicks Approve (optional note) or Reject (reason required). Stock moves only on approval.
If you request more than is in stock, the app warns you with the available and requested numbers and asks you to confirm. A rejection is final — a rejected record cannot be approved later.

6. Maintenance

Raise and track repair / maintenance requests for your unit. Every request is routed automatically by type — Computer / IT goes to the district's Computer/IT Staff, Vehicle to the MTO Staff, and everything else to the District Admin — and moves through Pending → Under Process → Completed.

  • Top button — + New Request.
  • Filters — search, All Status (Pending / Under Process / Completed), All Types (Plumber, Electrician, Carpenter, Mason / Civil Work, Computer / IT, Vehicle, Other), a date range, and Clear.
  • Columns — Request ID, Request To, Requesting Unit, Request Type, Description, Status, Created At, Action. Newest first.
  • Row actions — Process (on a pending request you may act on), Approve (on an under-process one), and Details (always). Where you cannot act, the cell shows Awaiting admin or In progress.

Raising a request

  1. Click + New Request.
  2. Choose the Maintenance Type (required). Picking Other reveals a box to type the type yourself.
  3. Check Request To — it fills in automatically based on the type you picked.
  4. Write the Description (required, at least 10 characters).
  5. Optionally attach Photos using Upload or Camera.
  6. Click Submit Request. You get a new ID like MR-000001.

Working a request

  1. Click Process on a Pending row, add an optional remark, then Proceed to Process — status becomes Under Process.
  2. Click Approve on an Under Process row, add an optional completion remark, then Approve & Complete — status becomes Completed.
  3. Click Details on any row to see the full record, its photos, and the Timeline of who did what and when.
A pending request can be processed only by the person it is addressed to; an under-process request can be approved only by the requesting unit. Completed requests can no longer be changed. The Developer Admin has read-only access here.
You can attach up to 4 photos to a maintenance request. The Camera button falls back to a file picker automatically if no camera is available.

7. Demands

Formal requests for material from another district or post.

  • + Raise Demand — select item, quantity, category, condition, urgency (normal / urgent / critical), reason, and the destination district + location.
  • Summary cards — Total / Pending / Approved / Rejected; click any one to jump to that filtered list.
  • Search & filter — by text, status, and date range.
  • Reviewing (on the receiving side): open a pending demand, see the full details, then Approve & Submit or Reject (a rejection reason is required).
  • Download / Print — PDF, Excel, Word or Print.
When you raise a demand, the target district's admin gets a notification under the 🔔 bell, and a red dot appears on the Demands icon until it is reviewed.

8. Inspections

Records of asset inspections (Routine / Special / Annual).

  • + New Inspection — item/asset, inspected by, type, date, location, findings, recommendations, status.
  • Summary cards — Total / Completed / Pending / Overdue; click any one to filter.
  • Search & filter — by text, type, and status.
  • Download / Print — PDF, Excel, Word or Print.

9. Item Issued

Issue items to personnel and track returns. One rule governs the whole screen: Total Qty = Available + Issued + Damaged / Lost. You can never issue more than what is currently available, and quantities can never go negative.

  • Top buttons — + Issue Items, Import from Excel, Scan & Import, Scans.
  • Two tabs — Issued Items and Return History. (The item stock list lives on the Inventory screen now.)

Issued Items tab

  • Columns — S.No, Name, BELT No., Mobile No., Item, Category, Qty, Lost, Date, Time, Status, Actions. Click a heading to sort.
  • Filters — search, All Categories, All Items, Posting, All Ranks, All Status (Issued, Partially Returned, Partially Recovered, Returned, Lost, Scrap, Cancelled), a date range, and Clear.
  • Clicking the person's Name opens their full profile (section 10).

Return History tab

  • Columns — S.No, Person, BELT No., Item, Qty Returned, Condition, Return Date, Return Time, Received By, Remarks.
  • Filter by text, then Download / Print for PDF, Excel, Word or Print.

Issuing items

  1. Click + Issue Items at the top of the Item Issued screen (or the Inventory screen).
  2. In Person details, start typing a name or BELT number — matching personnel appear for instant fill. Otherwise enter name, post/rank, BELT number, and posting.
  3. Under Item details, pick a Category first — only then does the Item drop-down unlock, showing just the items in that category. Choose the item; balance chips show its Total / Available / Issued.
  4. Enter the item Quantity. You can issue items from different categories and quantities in one go: press + Add Item to add more rows (Category → Item → Qty each). Remove a row with the × in its corner.
  5. If any quantity exceeds the available stock, you get “Insufficient stock for … Only X units available.”
  6. Set date/time (usually today) and optional remarks, then press Issue Items — every row is issued together under one issue record.
After an issue is saved it is locked: you can only change the quantity. Person details, item, category, date, time and remarks can no longer be edited.

Editing, cancelling and returns

  • Edit on an active issue record — only the quantity can be changed (never below what has already been returned). Everything else is greyed out. Increasing the quantity re-checks available stock.
  • Cancel — closes the issue record and returns the remaining units to stock.
  • Return — enter the Return Quantity (partial returns are allowed) and choose the Condition on return: Good (adds the units back to available), Damaged, Lost or Other. Stock figures update immediately and the return appears in Return History.
Issued items respect the same role limits as the rest of the app: who can issue, edit, return and cancel is controlled by the user's role, and station/post users only ever see their own location's data.

10. Item Profile & Person Profile

Two full-history screens. Nothing is typed here — every row is computed from the existing issue records, stock history, demands and distributions.

Item Profile — everything about one item

  • How to open it: click the blue item name link in the Inventory table (also works in Consumable Items, Distribution, Demands and Reports), or use Actions ▸ View and then Open Full Profile.
  • Top row — a breadcrumb, a status badge (Available / Low / Fully Issued / Out of Stock), and chips for Item Code, Category, Unit, Location, District and Min Stock. Buttons: Edit Item (if you are allowed) and Adjust Stock.
  • Number cards — TOTAL, AVAILABLE, ISSUED, RETURNED, LOST, SCRAP, GOOD, DAMAGED, RECOVERED.
  • Sections — Item Information, Complete Transaction History, Item Activity Timeline, Uploaded Photos.
  • History columns — Date & Time, Transaction Type, Reference, From, To, Quantity, Condition, Balance, Performed By, Remarks. Click a heading to sort; click a row to see the previous and new balance.
  • Filters — search, All Types, All Conditions, date range, User / Unit, rows per page, and Export / Download (CSV, Excel, PDF, Print) for either the filtered rows or the complete history.
  • Photos — Upload Photo or Camera, click a thumbnail to zoom. Up to 20 photos, 8 MB each.

Person Profile — everything about one officer

  • How to open it: click the person's Name in Item Issued → Issued Items, or the Person in Return History.
  • Top row — breadcrumb, rank badge, and chips for Belt No., Posting, Mobile, District, Designation. Buttons: Edit Details and Issue Item.
  • Number cards — ISSUES, TOTAL ISSUED, RETURNED, PARTIAL RETURN, LOST, CURRENTLY HELD, DIFFERENT ITEMS, PENDING RETURNS, RECOVERED.
  • Sections — Active Holdings, Person Item History, Complete Issue / Return & Lost Audit, Photo History.
  • History columns — Date & Time, Item, Transaction Type, Quantity, Reference, Issued By, Processed By, Condition, Remark, Action. Item names here open that item's profile.
  • Filters — search, All Types, All Categories, All Items, All Conditions, date range, Clear Filters, and Export / Download.
Rows tagged “derived” are worked out by the app from current records — nobody entered them. A trailing SYSTEM SYNC row reconciles the running balance to the present stock figure.
You can only open profiles inside your own district (Developer Admin excepted). Mobile numbers read Restricted for non-admin users, and photos can be uploaded only by the owning unit or a District Admin.

11. Scan & Import (OCR)

Automatically read a filled application or receipt and let the app fill in an issue record for you — always with a human verification step before anything is saved. The button Scan & Import is on the Item Issued screen; Scans shows every document you have imported.

Supported formats

Images (JPG / PNG / WebP / BMP), PDF (text or scanned), Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX), and TXT / CSV. The reading is done in your browser — the file is not uploaded before you confirm it.

How it works

  1. Click Scan & Import, then click or drag a file onto the upload box.
  2. Press Scan Document. The app reads the document (progress is shown; the OCR model downloads once on first use) and extracts person details — Name, Post / Rank, BELT No., Posting — and the Items + Quantities by matching against your item list.
  3. The Review & Verify screen opens with every field pre-filled and editable. Correct anything the computer misread, add or remove item rows, and check the Raw extracted text panel to compare with the original.
  4. Only when you press Verify & Insert Data is anything saved: the person is created (if new), the issue appears in Issued Items, and item stock is reduced — exactly as if you issued it manually.
  5. The original document is stored with the record. Open Scans → View to see or download the original file, the person it was issued to, the items, date, and who verified it.
Items are matched by name, so scanned items must already exist in your inventory to be recognised. A quantity that exceeds available stock is blocked at verification, just like a manual issue. Nothing is ever inserted without your confirmation.

12. Reports

Three ready-made reports. Reports respect your role — for example a post or station user only ever sees their own location's data in every report and export.

  • Stock Report — current stock position by item, with clickable summary cards: Total Items, In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock, Good Units, Poor Units, and Damaged Units. Click any card to open a detail table of exactly those items.
  • Category Breakdown — items grouped by category with quantities.
  • Location Report — stock grouped by location / station.
  • Search — type in the search box to filter the current report instantly.
  • Columns ▾ — tick or untick columns to show only what you need, and drag column headings to reorder them.
  • Download / Print ▾ — export as PDF, Excel or Word, or print. Every report table has a bold Total row, and it is included in the export and print.

Admin tools on this screen

  • Audit Log (District Admin and Developer Admin) — who changed what, and when.
  • Backup (JSON), Backup (Excel) and Restore Data (Developer Admin only) — save a full copy of the system data, or load one back.

13. Notifications (🔔 Bell)

Click the bell in the top bar to open the notifications panel. The red number shows unread items.

  • You are notified about access requests, password reset requests, new demands, new distributions and their approval, new consumable distribution requests, and maintenance requests (new, under process, completed).
  • Use Mark all read to clear the counter, and View all to see the complete list.
  • An admin can act directly on an access request from the panel itself — Approve or Reject — while it is still pending.
  • Clicking a notification takes you straight to the relevant screen (maintenance, demands, inventory, users).
Notification scope: the Developer Admin sees notifications across all districts (30 most recent); other users see only their own district, filtered to their own location or user.

14. Users, Districts & Locations

These screens live under the gold Manage ▾ menu in the top bar. What you can see depends on your role.

  • Manage Districts (Developer Admin) — add new districts; search by name, code or headquarters.
  • Manage Users (Developer Admin) — two lists: District Admins and Developer Admins. From a District Admin row you can open Users to see the non-admin accounts under them, or Edit / Delete the account.
  • Manage Locations (District Admin) — add and remove your district's locations and stations. The district and its headquarters cannot be deleted.
  • Manage Users (District Admin) — create, edit or remove accounts inside your own district. Columns: Username, Display Name, Mobile, Role, District, Location.
  • The District selector in the top bar lets the Developer Admin switch which district is being viewed; every other user is locked to their own district.
Only create accounts for people who genuinely need access. Admin accounts are powerful — never create admin accounts for general staff.

16. Good Practices & Language

  • Language — the हिन्दी / English button in the top bar switches the whole app between Hindi and English. Your choice is remembered for next time.
  • Always press Logout on a shared device.
  • Keep your password private — it is stored hashed and cannot be read by anyone.
  • Use Forgot Password? on the login screen if you need a reset; the admin is notified automatically.
  • Wrong login? Passwords are checked server-side; repeated failures are rate-limited for your protection.
  • Back up local-data\db.json regularly, or use Backup in Reports if you are the Developer Admin.
